About
=====
I am a PhD researcher in energy economics, currently visiting the [Energy Policy Research Group (EPRG)](https://www.jbs.cam.ac.uk/faculty-research/centres/energy-policy-research-group/) at Judge Business School, University of Cambridge. I am also pursuing my PhD at the [Center for Energy and Environmental Policy Research, Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT-CEEP)](https://ceep.bit.edu.cn/english/index.htm).

My research focuses on household energy transitions, residential energy affordability, and policy design for low-carbon energy systems. I am particularly interested in how energy-transition policies affect households differently across income groups, age structures, regions, and climate conditions. My work combines household-level microdata, cost-benefit assessment, policy evaluation, and energy-economic modelling.

Research Interests
==================
- Household energy transition and residential decarbonization
- Energy affordability, inequality, and distributional impacts
- Energy policy evaluation, cost-benefit analysis, and incentive design
